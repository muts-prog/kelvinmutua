About

Statistics, AI, and real-world impact

I'm a Data Scientist, Statistician, and AI Researcher pursuing a PhD in Computer Science at the University of Nairobi, with a background in applied statistics and data analytics. My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistics, and sustainable development.

I'm particularly interested in developing practical, data-driven solutions to challenges in agriculture, food safety, public health, climate resilience, and development. My current research focuses on AI-driven risk prediction, early-warning systems, and decision-support tools, applying machine learning and statistical methods to real-world African development challenges.
