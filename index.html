Kelvin Mutua Murungi

Data Scientist, Statistician & AI Researcher — PhD Candidate, Computer Science, University of Nairobi

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Nairobi, supported by the Danida Scholarship (Reclaim_KE). I hold a Master of Science in Applied Statistics from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology, and a BSc in Applied Statistics with Computing from the University of Kabianga.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, statistics, and monitoring & evaluation. I focus on AI-driven risk prediction, early-warning systems, and decision-support tools for agriculture, food safety, public health, and climate resilience across Africa.
